ELAH WARRIOR · Credits

ELAH WARRIOR is a playable 3D Bible combat game made for Blossom, in six levels: 1 David, from Elah to Adullam · 2 Abram rescues Lot · 3 Benaiah · 4 Deborah and Barak · 5 Samson · 6 David’s mighty men.

Characters and animation

  • Bodies, faces, eyes, hair and beards: Universal Base Characters by Quaternius (CC0, public domain).
  • Animations: Universal Animation Library by Quaternius (CC0, public domain).
  • Costumes, armour, weapons, wings, the lion, horses and chariots, every world (the valley of Elah, the wadi and the cave of Adullam, Lehi, the house of Dagon, the night camp at Dan, the highlands of Moab and the pit, the plain of the river Kishon, the fields of Bethlehem), the sky, weather, effects and colour grade: made in code for this game.
  • Faces (blinking, brows, jaw, a shape for each character), army helmets and headdresses, scale armour and the slow-motion finishing blow: made in code for this game, no extra files.
  • Narrator: a voice for the story cards plays only from recordings placed in the game (Settings › Narrator). No AI voices.

Sound effects

  • Hits, armour, wood and coins: Impact Sounds and RPG Audio by Kenney (CC0, public domain).
  • Other effects (sling, wind, roars, the temple collapse): synthesised live in the browser. No recordings of people, no AI voices.

Engine

  • three.js (MIT licence).
