ELAH · Credits

ELAH: Rise of the Shepherd is a playable demo of a Bible combat game (Level 1: David, Level 2: Samson, Level 3: Abraham rescues Lot), made for Blossom.

  • Characters (bodies, faces, eyes, hair and beards): Universal Base Characters by Quaternius (CC0, public domain).
  • Animations: Universal Animation Library by Quaternius (CC0, public domain). Costumes, armour, weapons, wings, the valley, Lehi, the temple, the night camp at Dan (tents, fires, torches, stars and moon), the snowy highlands of Moab, the pit, the falling snow, the lion, the plain of the river Kishon with mount Tabor, the rain and storm, the chariots of iron and their horses, and Jael’s tent are made in code for this demo.
  • 3D engine: three.js (MIT licence).
  • Sound effects and battle music (war drums, low strings): synthesised live in the browser for this demo. No recordings, no AI voices.
  • Scripture: King James Version (public domain), including 1 Samuel 17; Judges 15–16; Deuteronomy 31:6; Isaiah 40:31; Psalm 91:11; Genesis 14:9–20; Genesis 15:1; Psalm 3:3.
