## Made in code for this game

- **Costumes and props:** armour, helmets, crowns, veils, shields, spears, swords, bows, slings, staffs and wings, built as meshes attached to bones, plus a region-paint shader.
- **Worlds:**
  - the valley of Elah, the wilderness wadi and the cave of Adullam
  - Lehi and the temple of Dagon
  - the night camp at Dan (tents, fires, torches, moon and stars)
  - the snowy highlands of Moab, the pit and the lion
  - the plain of the river Kishon with mount Tabor, the rain and storm, the chariots of iron and their horses, and Jael's tent
  - the harvest fields and the well of Bethlehem
- **Sound effects and battle music** (war drums, low strings, pads): synthesised live in the browser with Web Audio. There are no recordings and no AI voices.
