# ELAH WARRIOR · Credits

Six levels: 1 David (Elah to Adullam), 2 Abram rescues Lot, 3 Benaiah, 4 Deborah and Barak, 5 Samson, 6 David's mighty men.

- Universal Animation Library by Quaternius, CC0.
